Choose what moves

Tick what moves and pick how it moves.

HowWhat happens
copy wholeThe folder is copied into the encrypted bundle as it is.
clone again + bundle/dirtyThe repository is cloned again on the new laptop; local commits, stashes and uncommitted files travel in the bundle.
clone againOnly cloned again: nothing of it is in the bundle.
by handNot moved: it is on your by-hand list.

A client repository with a remote stays out of the bundle: push its work first, then clone it again. One without a remote goes into the separate client part, for your Accenture OneDrive only.

Applications show how they are installed again: winget, a vendor download that runs only when its signature is valid, or Software Center.

Printers and Wi-Fi are not moved: they are checked on the new laptop.
