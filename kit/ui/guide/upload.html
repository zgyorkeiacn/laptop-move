Upload and download

The mover does not upload anything. You put the bundle on your own Accenture OneDrive, where you can reach it from the new laptop.

FilesWhere they go
Data parts, kit zip, checksums, manifestYour Accenture OneDrive only.
The client partYour Accenture OneDrive only. Never a client's OneDrive, never a personal drive.
The passwordNowhere. Keep it in your password manager.

Steps

  1. Open OneDrive in your browser, signed in with your Accenture account.
  2. Create a folder named move.
  3. Drag every file of the bundle folder in, the kit zip too.
  4. Wait until each file shows a green tick. Large files can take long: leave the laptop plugged in and awake.
Check the account. Your computer may also hold a client's OneDrive folder. The upload target must say Accenture. If you are not sure, upload through the browser, signed in with your Accenture account.

On the new laptop

  1. Sign in to your Accenture OneDrive in the browser and open the move folder.
  2. Download every file into one new local folder, for example C:\move. Do not use a synced OneDrive folder as the work place.
  3. Check that every file has its full size before you go on (see Troubleshooting if a checksum fails later).
