Reading the report

When the restore ends, the Final report screen shows the result, and Open the report as HTML opens the same report as a page you can keep or print.

The three counters

TagMeaningWhat you do
doneRestored and checked.Nothing.
to do by handSkipped, not moved on purpose, or needs you.Do it from Your own steps now.
failedA part is damaged or missing, an app was refused, or a conflict kept your file.See Troubleshooting.

The sections

  • Parts: each part with its size and whether its SHA-256 matched.
  • Applications: installed, already there, listed for Software Center, or refused.
  • Repositories: cloned, whether the local-commit bundle was fetched, and what was put back.
  • Folders and settings: where each went, how many files moved, how many were replaced.
  • Files that already existed: kept as they were. The old laptop's copy stays in the work folder; run the restore again with Overwrite to replace them (each replaced file is backed up first).
  • Warnings: things to know, such as a missing client rules file.
  • Your own steps now: the by-hand list, the printer check, the Wi-Fi check, signing in to Edge and Chrome, and Git signing in on first clone.

After the report

  1. Work through Your own steps now and tick off your by-hand list.
  2. Clone the client repositories you pushed before the move.
  3. Print one page on the secure printer and open one repository to be sure.
  4. Delete the bundle from OneDrive and the work folder from the new laptop when you are satisfied. Keep the password until then.
