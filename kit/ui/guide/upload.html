Upload

The mover does not upload. Put the files on your Accenture OneDrive yourself.

  1. Open OneDrive in your browser and create a folder named move.
  2. Drag the .7z parts and the kit zip into it.
  3. Wait until each file shows a green tick.

The client part goes on your Accenture OneDrive only. Never put it on a client's OneDrive or on a personal drive, and never upload the password.
