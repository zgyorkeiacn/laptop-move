The by-hand list

Secrets are never copied by the mover. Move each one the safe way for what it is, or create it again on the new laptop.

  • .env files, .npmrc files with tokens, the .ssh folder and cloud command line credentials: create them again, or move them the way your team prescribes.
  • Browser saved logins: sign in on the new laptop and let the browser sync.
  • Printers: secure print queues come from the company setup on the new laptop. Check that one page prints.
  • Wi-Fi: no profile or key is packed. Check that the office network is there, and join other networks by hand.
