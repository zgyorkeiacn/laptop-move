The report

When the restore ends the page shows what was done, what is left for you by hand and what failed, section by section. Open the full report as HTML, or print it.

  • Done: nothing more to do.
  • To do by hand: follow the detail, then tick it off on the by-hand list.
  • Failed: read the detail. A part that failed its checksum is downloaded again; an app whose signature did not match is installed from the vendor's own page.
